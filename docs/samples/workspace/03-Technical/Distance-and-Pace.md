# Distance and pace

For a walk of $d = 9.6$ km that takes $t = 3.2$ hours, the average speed is $v = d/t$.

$$
v = \frac{d}{t} = \frac{9.6}{3.2} = 3 \text{ km/h}
$$

## Split times

The route has three measured legs of $t_1 = 52$, $t_2 = 68$, and $t_3 = 72$ minutes.

$$
T = \sum_{i=1}^{3} t_i = 192 \text{ min}
$$

## Grade

A section rises $\Delta h = 48$ m over a horizontal distance of $\Delta x = 600$ m.

$$
g = 100 \cdot \frac{\Delta h}{\Delta x} = 8\%
$$
