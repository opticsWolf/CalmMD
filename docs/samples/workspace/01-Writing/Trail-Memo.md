# Trail memo

## Why we are going

The walk is a chance to test a route before inviting a larger group. We will leave enough time for short stops, note which signs are easy to miss, and compare the descent with the lowland alternative.

## What to tell the group

**Meet at the station at 09:15.** Bring water, a warm layer, and something for lunch. The ridge is optional. In _poor visibility_ we will stay in the valley and meet the same return train. The earlier 08:40 departure is ~~cancelled~~. Save the route under the label `RIDGE-A`.

> The aim is an unhurried walk with enough daylight left to change our minds.
